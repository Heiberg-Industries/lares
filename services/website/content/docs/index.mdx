Lares is a self-hosted fleet of named agents for the everyday work of a small business. This guide introduces the console, agents and installation model.

Lares is an **experimental alpha**. The code is public; availability and pricing for hosted and managed work are still being worked out.

## Find your way

- [The console](/docs/console): see your agents and review the work that needs you.
- [Agents and permissions](/docs/agents): understand definitions, access and model keys.
- [Installation](/docs/installation): learn the evaluation path and its current limits.
- [Privacy](/docs/privacy): understand the boundary between this website and your installation.

## Three ways in

**Domus** means running Lares on your own server. **Villa** is help with hosting and ongoing care. **Familia** is managed work, starting with a conversation about the results you need.

[Get in touch](/#contact) to discuss a setup, or [explore the code](https://github.com/Heiberg-Industries/lares).
