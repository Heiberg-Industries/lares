## Marketing website and documentation

Heiberg Industries operates lares.is. With your permission, this website uses PostHog's EU service to understand visits, navigation, documentation use and contact or booking actions. Analytics is off until you accept it. Use **Analytics preferences** at the bottom of the screen to change your choice.

We measure page paths, referring website domains, campaign labels, section views, reading progress and the actions you take. Documentation search tracking records search use and result selection, not the words you type. We do not intentionally send email addresses, booking form fields or URL query strings to analytics. Session replay and automatic form capture are disabled.

Accepting analytics enables a browser identifier to connect visits. Consent choices are managed by Heiberg Industries’ shared service at consent.heiberg.co, with a local preference and a server-side consent record. Declining keeps PostHog off. Theme preferences are also stored locally.

## Contact and booking

Email opens your own mail application. Booking uses the Heiberg calendar service, which needs the information you provide to arrange your appointment. That service has its own privacy notice. Opening the booking window loads it only when you ask to book.

## Your Lares installation

Website analytics is separate from the self-hosted console and agent runtime. This website's PostHog integration is not included in those applications. Your installation's data remains subject to your configuration and the providers you choose.
