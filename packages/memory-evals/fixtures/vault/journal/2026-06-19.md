# 2026-06-19

Vestbrygga is paused. Publicly it's being framed as "waiting on scheduling"
so nobody involved looks bad, but the actual reason is that their bank
pulled back on the financing for the wider scheme after a valuation came in

bigger plan, and without the rest of the financing in place there's no
reason for them to move on just this one building yet.

Told Priya to stop billing hours against it until we hear more. Not going
to mention the financing detail to anyone outside the two of us unless
Fjord Eiendom says it themselves.
