# 2026-06-11

Walked Havnelager with Erik Dahl this morning before the site got too hot
to be up on the scaffold. He confirmed what the drawings suggested about
the south wall, but the reason turned out to be more specific than "it
needs new steel" — there used to be an old chimney flue running through
that section, and the masonry around it has been quietly rotting from the
inside for decades. He thinks it's been structurally marginal since well
before we bought the building, not something that happened on our watch.

Priya is going to revise the permit drawings to show the flue removal
explicitly, since the municipality will ask about it otherwise.

Otherwise a quiet day — Camilla confirmed Friday's photo drop for Runde
looked good, nothing flagged.
