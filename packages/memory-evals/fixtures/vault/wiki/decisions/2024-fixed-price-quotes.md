# Pricing model: fixed-price quotes

Decided to quote every job — new build and renovation alike — as a single
fixed price rather than time-and-materials, after two clients complained
about not knowing the final number until the invoice landed.

Superseded 2026-06 — renovation work moved back to time-and-materials; see
the newer decision note for why.
