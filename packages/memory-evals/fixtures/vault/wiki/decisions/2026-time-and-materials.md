# Pricing model: time-and-materials for renovation work

Reverses the 2024 move to fixed-price quotes, but only for renovation and
retrofit work — new-build jobs stay fixed-price. Renovation jobs keep
turning up hidden conditions behind old walls (the Lien Bakeri counter
rebuild found rotten joists nobody could have priced in advance), and
carrying that risk on a fixed number was costing more than the certainty
was worth.

New builds — the Nordkyst spa wing, Havnelager — are unaffected: full
drawings and a known structure make a fixed number fair to both sides
there.
