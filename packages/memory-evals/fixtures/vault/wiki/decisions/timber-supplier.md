# Preferred timber supplier: Lindqvist Sagbruk

Consolidated all structural and cladding timber purchasing with one mill —
Lindqvist Sagbruk — instead of splitting orders across three yards. Oskar
gives us a better rate than list price on orders placed at least four weeks
out, and having one relationship means one person to chase on a late
delivery rather than three.

Order at least a month ahead through the May–August stretch; their lead
time can run longer in that window.
