# Subcontractor vetting policy

Any subcontractor working on-site for the first time needs proof of
insurance and a reference from one other job before they're booked — no
exceptions, even for a one-day job. Came out of a near-miss in 2025 with an
electrical sub who turned out to be uninsured.

Ingrid keeps the reference checks; Thomas keeps the insurance certificates
on file.
