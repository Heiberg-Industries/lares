# Lien Bakeri — shopfront renovation

New windows, a rebuilt counter and a repainted facade at the main Lien
Bakeri location. Marte Lien insists the bakery stays open through the whole
job — every noisy step is scheduled early morning or overnight, never during
trading hours.

Thomas Berge is running this one largely solo while Ingrid is on leave.
