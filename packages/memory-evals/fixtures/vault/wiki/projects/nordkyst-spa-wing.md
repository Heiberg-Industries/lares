# Nordkyst Hotell — spa wing extension

A roughly 180 m² single-storey extension off the west side of the hotel,
adding two treatment rooms, a sauna and a small relaxation lounge. Erik
Solheim is the day-to-day contact; anything over NOK 150,000 needs the hotel
board's sign-off, which typically adds two weeks.

Currently framed and roofed, waiting on the sauna supplier's install slot.
Quoted at NOK 4.2M all in, including the spa fit-out fixtures.
